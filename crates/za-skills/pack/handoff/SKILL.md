# Handoff

Leave the work so that someone with no memory of this session can continue it without guessing.

## Write
1. Goal and the definition of done, in the user's words.
2. State: what is finished and verified (with the check), what is in progress, what has not started.
3. Changes: files touched, branches, commits, anything left uncommitted.
4. Decisions and their reasons, including approaches tried and rejected.
5. Open questions and who can answer them.
6. Next step: the exact first action, with the command or file.
7. Risks: what may break, what is fragile, what needs care.

## Where
Look in AGENTS.md and docs/ for a place meant for status or handoff notes, and use it. If the work follows a plan, for example a file in docs/plans/, add the note to that plan. If the project has neither, give the note in your final reply instead of creating new folders for it. Keep it short enough to read in a minute.

## Stop when
A reader could take the next step from the note alone.

## Do not
- Claim progress you did not check.
- Leave out failures and dead ends: they save the next person time.
- Paste long logs. Quote the line that matters.
